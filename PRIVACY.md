# Postbird privacy policy

Postbird does not collect data and does not send anything to its developers.

- **No tracking.** The app has no analytics, no account and no crash reporting.
- **Your connections stay on your Mac.** Saved connection details are stored locally in the app's own container. Postbird talks only to the PostgreSQL servers you tell it to connect to.
- **AI assistant (optional).** It is off until you configure a provider. When you ask it something, Postbird sends schema metadata (table and column names, types, keys) to the provider you chose. It sends row data only if you turn that on. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.
- **Contact.** Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/Paxa/postbird/issues.
